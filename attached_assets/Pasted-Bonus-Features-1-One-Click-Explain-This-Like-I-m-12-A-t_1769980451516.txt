Bonus Features 
1️⃣ One-Click “Explain This Like I’m 12”
A toggle that re-explains any clause in:
•	Super simple language
•	With a real-world example
“This means if you quit early, you might owe them money.”
Why it hits:
People don’t want legal English → they want understanding.
________________________________________
2️⃣ Visual Risk Heatmap
Instead of only text, show:
•	Contract sections color-coded:
o	🟢 Safe
o	🟡 Caution
o	🔴 Dangerous
Users can see risk instantly.
Why it hits:
Visual clarity = instant trust.
________________________________________
3️⃣ “What If?” Simulator
User clicks:
•	“What if I cancel early?”
•	“What if they sue me?”
•	“What if I miss a payment?”
The agent answers using only the contract text.
Why it hits:
This turns fear into foresight.
________________________________________
4️⃣ Contract Expiry Radar
A passive feature:
•	Scans signed contracts
•	Alerts:
o	“This auto-renews in 14 days.”
o	“This termination window closes soon.”
Why it hits:
People lose money because they forget — this saves them silently.
________________________________________
5️⃣ Legal Tone Switch
When generating negotiation responses, user selects:
•	Polite
•	Firm
•	Assertive
•	Lawyer-like
Why it hits:
People fear sounding “wrong” in negotiations. This gives confidence.
________________________________________
6️⃣ “Is This Normal?” Button
For any clause, user taps:
“Is this normal?”
The agent answers carefully:
•	“This clause is commonly seen in X contracts.”
•	“This clause is more aggressive than typical.”
(No legal claims — just pattern awareness.)
Why it hits:
This is one of the most common legal anxieties.
________________________________________
7️⃣ Smart Clause Bookmarking
Users can bookmark clauses like:
•	“Termination”
•	“Liability”
•	“Non-compete”
Over time, the agent learns:
“You always check termination clauses first.”
Why it hits:
Personalization without creepiness.
________________________________________
8️⃣ Voice Mode (Explain It to Me)
User taps a button and hears:
•	A calm, human voice explanation of risks
Why it hits:
Accessibility, emotional comfort, and premium feel.
________________________________________
9️⃣ Share-Safe Summary
One-click generate:
•	A short, non-legal summary you can send to:
o	Partner
o	Co-founder
o	Parent
o	Spouse
Why it hits:
People discuss contracts socially.
________________________________________
🔟 Trust Seal (Psychological Weapon)
After analysis, show:
“Reviewed by Contract Advocate AI™
Grounded in your document.
No laws invented.”
Why it hits:
Trust messaging is half the product in legal tech.
________________________________________
🧠 Why These Matter
None of these are “features for features’ sake.”
They:
•	Reduce fear
•	Increase clarity
•	Increase emotional trust
•	Make the app feel protective, not technical
This is how users go from:
“This is helpful”
to:
“I won’t sign anything without this.”
