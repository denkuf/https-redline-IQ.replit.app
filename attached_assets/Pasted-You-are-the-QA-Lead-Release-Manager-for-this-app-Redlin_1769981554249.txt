You are the QA Lead + Release Manager for this app: RedlineIQ (Contract Translator).

Your job:

Audit the entire codebase and running app to ensure all core requirements from the original product idea are implemented.

Identify missing features, broken flows, weak/unsafe AI behaviors, incomplete UI actions, and edge-case failures.

Run the full test suite and also conduct manual end-to-end tests.

Output a QA report and then fix issues one-by-one until all tests pass.

Product Requirements (Must Match)

RedlineIQ is a contract translator and advocate-grade analyzer. It must:

Accept contract input via PDF upload, photo/image upload, and pasted text

Extract readable text reliably from PDF/images

Provide outputs in layers:

Plain-English summary

Key Terms table (payment, term, renewal, cancellation/termination, liability/indemnity, dispute resolution, governing law if present, confidentiality/IP if present)

Risk flags with severity (Low/Medium/High), explanation, exact clause quote, clause reference, and confidence score

Never invent laws or facts; must be grounded in the contract text

Must support clarification questions when uncertain/missing key information

Provide “My Contracts” history, search, view results, delete

Export results (PDF and/or email-friendly text)

Privacy controls: delete a contract permanently, purge data

Clean UX: no dead buttons, no broken routes, safe empty states

Non-Negotiable AI Safety Rules

The AI must not claim legal advice or cite laws/statutes as fact.

Every risk flag must include:

Clause quote (short excerpt)

A reference/anchor (section heading or approximate location)

If the AI can’t locate support in text → it must say “Not found in document.”

If confidence < 0.70 → must label as “uncertain/ambiguous” and suggest professional review.
