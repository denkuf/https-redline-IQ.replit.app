RedlineIQ is an AI-powered contract intelligence and life clarity assistant designed to help users understand documents, screenshots, policies, agreements, and important decisions with confidence.

Whether you are reviewing a contract, analyzing a subscription, evaluating a job offer, understanding a policy, or trying to make sense of a confusing screenshot or email, RedlineIQ helps simplify complex information into clear, actionable guidance.

KEY FEATURES

• Screenshot Intelligence
Upload screenshots, photos, PDFs, or text and instantly receive:

* Plain-English explanations
* Risk highlights
* Important deadlines
* Recommended next steps
* Smart reminders

• Contract & Redline Analysis
Review agreements, detect edits and redlines, identify negotiation risks, and understand important clauses before signing.

• “What’s the Catch?” Mode
Analyze offers, proposals, subscriptions, policies, and terms to uncover hidden risks, vague wording, pressure tactics, and unfavorable conditions.

• Ask-Anytime Advocate Chat
Get AI-powered guidance and decision support for contracts, negotiations, obligations, policies, messages, and everyday situations.

• Personal Guardian Monitoring
Track renewals, recurring obligations, cancellation windows, deadlines, and important responsibilities so nothing important slips through the cracks.

• Decision Support Engine
Compare options, understand consequences, and evaluate potential risks before making important personal or business decisions.

WHO IT’S FOR

RedlineIQ is built for:

* Professionals
* Entrepreneurs
* Freelancers
* Parents
* Renters
* Small business owners
* Anyone who wants clarity before making important decisions

IMPORTANT NOTICE

RedlineIQ provides informational and educational assistance only and does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or professional legal services. Users should consult a qualified attorney for legal matters requiring professional advice.

Before you sign, upload, respond, or decide — run it through RedlineIQ.
