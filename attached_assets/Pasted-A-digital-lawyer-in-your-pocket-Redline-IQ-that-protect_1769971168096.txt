🧠 Core Promise
This agent must behave like:
•	A top-tier contract attorney
•	A ruthless risk auditor
•	A calm human explainer
•	A personal advocate
It must:
1.	Translate legal language into human English
2.	Identify real risks
3.	Expose hidden traps
4.	Say who this contract benefits
5.	Tell the user what to change
6.	Answer:
“Should I sign this?”
And it must do so with near-zero hallucination. You cannot make a mistake
V1 Goal
Deliver a high-accuracy, grounded contract explainer that users can trust.
V1 Modules (Required)
1.	Upload & Input
o	Upload: PDF, DOC/DOCX, JPG, PNG
o	Paste contract text
o	“Scan/photo” upload (image → text extraction)
2.	Contract Viewer
o	Show extracted text clearly
o	Highlight detected sections/clauses
o	Allow user to select text and click “Explain this”
3.	Results Page (Layered Output)
Output must be in layers:
A) Plain-English Summary
o	What this contract is
o	Who is involved
o	Key obligations for user
o	Key obligations for other party
o	Dates/term (if found)
B) Key Terms Table
o	Payment/price
o	Term length
o	Cancellation/termination
o	Renewal
o	Liability/indemnity
o	Dispute resolution (arbitration/court)
o	Governing law (only if mentioned)
o	Confidentiality/IP (if present)
C) Risk Flags (Grounded)
For each risk:
o	Risk title
o	Severity: Low / Medium / High
o	Why it’s risky (plain English)
o	Exact clause quote (short excerpt) + clause reference (e.g., “Section 8.2” or nearest heading)
o	Confidence score (0–1)
4.	Clarifying Questions (When Needed)
If key info is missing or ambiguous, ask 1–3 short questions such as:
o	“Is this for you personally or a business?”
o	“What state/country are you in?” (optional for context, but do not apply law)
o	“Is your main concern cost, cancellation, or liability?”
5.	Export
o	Export summary + risk flags to PDF
o	Export to email-friendly text
6.	History
o	“My Contracts” list
o	Search by name/type/date
o	Delete contract permanently
7.	Settings
o	Privacy controls (delete/purge data)
o	Notification preferences (optional)
o	Disclaimer screen

V1 AI Rules (Critical)
The AI must follow these rules strictly:
1.	No law invention
o	The AI must not claim “this is illegal” or cite statutes.
o	It may say: “This clause may increase your risk because…”
2.	Grounding required
o	Every “risk flag” must reference the exact clause text.
o	If no clause supports it, it cannot be said.
3.	Uncertainty handling
o	If confidence < 0.70, the AI must say:
“I’m not fully certain—this is ambiguous; consider a professional review.”
4.	Safe output
o	Always show a disclaimer:
“I’m not a lawyer. This is informational, not legal advice.”
